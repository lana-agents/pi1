# The étale fundamental group of a scheme

In this repository we formalise the étale fundamental group of a connected scheme in Lean 4,
building on top of Lean's math library [mathlib4](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4).

The étale fundamental group `π₁ᵉᵗ(ξ)` of a connected scheme `X` at a geometric point `ξ` is defined
as the automorphism group of the fiber functor associated to `ξ`

We verify that if `X` is a connected scheme, the category of schemes finite étale over `X`
is a Galois category and taking geometric fibers over a geometric point is a fiber functor.

The main result is `AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteEtale.galoisCategory` in
[`Pi1.FundamentalGroup.Galois`](Pi1/FundamentalGroup/Galois.lean).

In particular, by the general framework of Galois categories, `π₁ᵉᵗ(ξ)` is a pro-finite group and
the fiber functor associated to `ξ` induces an equivalence of category with the category
of finite `π₁ᵉᵗ(ξ)`-sets.

**Étale fundamental groups of affine orbifolds** (`Pi1/Orbifold/`, moved from
`tempered-fundamental-groups`)

* `Pi1.Orbifold.etalePi1 R A Ω`: the automorphisms of the fibre functor on `A`-equivariant finite
  étale `R`-algebras, i.e. the étale fundamental group of `[Spec R / A]`. The fibre-functor
  topology is `Pi1.Orbifold.FibreAut`, and coded finite étale algebras are `EtaleCode` and
  `SemilinearAut`. The group is profinite (`etalePi1Profinite`). For trivial `A` it is
  isomorphic to Mathlib's `Aut (CommAlgCat.FiniteEtale.fiber R Ω)` (`etalePi1EquivAutFiber`).
* SGA 1 V.8.2 with a finite group action: `Pi1.Orbifold.GaloisData.equivPi1` gives
  `etalePi1 R A Ω ≃ₜ* GaloisPi1.pi1 H S`, the quotient of a Galois group by the closed normal
  subgroup generated by inertia. It is proved with induced covers (`Induced.lean`).
* `Algebra.smooth_of_isDedekindDomain` (`Pi1/RingTheory/Smooth/Dedekind.lean`): a Dedekind domain
  of finite type over a perfect field is smooth. Consequently the coordinate rings of
  normalizations of the `t`-line are smooth of dimension `1` (`Pi1.Orbifold.CoordRing.smooth`,
  `CoordRing.ringKrullDim_eq_one`, `GaloisData.smooth_R`, `GaloisData.ringKrullDim_R`).

**Orbicurves, cores and ramification** (`Pi1/Orbicurve/`)

* Affine orbicurves `AffOrbicurve k`, given by a coarse Dedekind ring and stabilizer orders, with
  their finite étale morphisms and `k`-cores (`IsCoreOf`), following Mochizuki, *The absolute
  anabelian geometry of canonical curves* ([CanLift]) §2.
* Once-punctured elliptic curves `AffOrbicurve.punctured E` and their `±1`-quotients
  `AffOrbicurve.hemi E`, and the statements `CanLift27` and `CanLift27Arith` of [CanLift]
  Prop. 2.7 (`Elliptic.lean`). They are **statements only** in this repository.
  `AffOrbicurve.canLift27_of_complex` (Lefschetz principle) proves `CanLift27` over every field of
  characteristic `0` from the statement over `ℂ`.
* Galois-theoretic fundamental groups `GaloisPi1.pi1` (`GaloisPi1.lean`), inertia groups of
  valuation subrings and their surjectivity in towers (`ValuationInertia.lean`).
* Étaleness via inertia (`AffOrbicurve.etale_iff_inertia`), cyclicity of tame inertia
  (`TameInertia.isCyclic_inertia`), and unramified maps into valuation rings
  (`Algebra.FormallyUnramified.algHom_eq_of_valuation`).
* Cores: pullbacks of finite étale covers (`AffOrbicurve.pullback_ofSubfield`), a criterion for
  cores (`AffOrbicurve.isCoreOf_of_subsingleton`), and invariance of ramification indices under
  extension of the constant field (`AffOrbicurve.ramificationIdx_bc`).

**Functoriality of `π₁ᵉᵗ` and surjectivity** (`Pi1/FundamentalGroup/Functoriality.lean`,
`Pi1/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Galois/FiberAutMap.lean`)

* `PreGaloisCategory.fiberAutMap e : Aut F →* Aut G` for `e : H ⋙ F ≅ G` (whiskering with `H`,
  conjugated by `e`), continuous (`continuous_fiberAutMap`), functorial (`fiberAutMap_congr`,
  `fiberAutMap_id`, `fiberAutMap_comp`); `FiberFunctor.ofIso`; SGA 1 V.6.9 (Galois-theoretic
  part): `toAut_comp_surjective`, `fiberAutMap_surjective` (if `H` preserves connected objects,
  `Aut F → Aut G` is surjective).
* For `f : Y ⟶ X` and geometric points `ξ`, `η` with `ξ ≫ f = η`:
  `FiniteEtale.pullbackFiberIso f h : pullback f ⋙ fiber ξ ≅ fiber η` and the continuous
  homomorphism `FiniteEtale.fundamentalGroupMap f h : π₁ᵉᵗ(ξ) →ₜ* π₁ᵉᵗ(η)`, with
  `fundamentalGroupMap_id` and `fundamentalGroupMap_comp`.
* `FiniteEtale.isConnected_iff_connectedSpace`: the connected objects of `FiniteEtale X` are the
  finite étale `X`-schemes with connected total space.
* SGA 1 V.6.9: `FiniteEtale.fundamentalGroupMap_surjective` (`X`, `Y` connected and `f^*`
  preserves connected objects ⇒ `π₁ᵉᵗ(Y) → π₁ᵉᵗ(X)` surjective), and the geometric form
  `fundamentalGroupMap_surjective_of_geometricallyConnected` for `f` universally open with
  geometrically connected fibres (Mathlib's `GeometricallyConnected`, `UniversallyOpen`).

**Profinite surface groups** (`Pi1/Surface/`, `Pi1/Profinite/`)

* `Pi1.SurfaceGroup g r`: `Π_{g,r} = ⟨a₁, b₁, …, a_g, b_g, c₁, …, c_r | ∏ [aᵢ, bᵢ] ∏ cⱼ = 1⟩`
  (conventions of `generalized-albanese`), `Pi1.ProfiniteSurfaceGroup g r = Π̂_{g,r}`;
  `SurfaceGroup.freeEquiv : Π_{g,r+1} ≃* F(a, b, c_{<r})`, `profiniteFreeEquiv`, and
  `oneOneEquiv : Π_{1,1} ≃* F₂`, `profiniteOneOneEquiv : Π̂_{1,1} ≃ₜ* F̂₂`.
* `Pi1.Profinite.liftFinite`: continuous extensions of homomorphisms to finite groups along
  `G → Ĝ`; they separate points (`eq_one_of_forall_liftFinite`); `completionMap`,
  `completionCongr`.
* **Slimness** (`Pi1.Profinite.IsSlim`: every open subgroup has trivial centraliser):
  `isSlim_completion_freeGroup` (free profinite groups of rank `≥ 2`, by a wreath-product
  argument), hence `SurfaceGroup.isSlim` and `SurfaceGroup.center_eq_bot` for hyperbolic `(g, r)`
  with `r ≥ 1`. The closed case `r = 0` is not proved here.
* **Cuspidal inertia**: `SurfaceGroup.cuspInertia j : ℤ̂ →* Π̂_{g,r}` (`k ↦ cⱼ ^ k`) is injective
  (`cuspInertia_injective`, for `r ≥ 2` or `g ≥ 1`), its image is the closed subgroup
  `inertia j = ⟨cⱼ⟩‾` (`range_cuspInertia`), and `cuspInertiaEquiv : ℤ̂ ≃ₜ* inertia j`.
